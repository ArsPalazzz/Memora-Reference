### Rhetoric

**Credibility** - the quality of being trusted, believed, or convincing.
**Virtue** – a morally good quality or behavior; moral excellence.
**Debunked claim** – a statement or belief that has been proven false or misleading through evidence or reasoning.
**Inherently** – in a permanent, essential, or natural way; by its very nature.
**To rally people for peace** – to gather, unite, or motivate people in support of peace.
**To incite to war** – to provoke or urge others to engage in war or violent conflict.
