# Do vs make

**do** — выполнять действие **без** физического результата (работа, обязанности, повторяющиеся задачи).  
**make** — **создавать** что-то, что можно увидеть или заметить (еда, решения, звуки, деньги).

## Do — вспомогательный глагол

В Present Simple — вопросы и отрицания:

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- What **do** you want?
- **Do** you need anything?
- He **doesn't** have a car.
```

Замена глагола, когда смысл ясен, или общие активности:

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- She doesn't like it, but I **do**.
- I've got so much to **do**.
- I need to **do** something about this problem.
- What can I **do** to help you?
```

## Do — коллокации (без физического результата)

### Study and work

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- I'll **do** my homework after dinner.
- Joe **does** a lot of work in the evenings.
- He hasn't **done** his report.
- I have a few assignments to **do**.
- I **do** some business in Japan.
- You **did** a really good job.
- I am **doing** a course.
- What **do** you **do**? _(= What's your occupation?)_
```

### Housework and repetitive tasks

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- I **do** the dishes every evening.
- Alice **does** the laundry when she gets home.
- I'll **do** the shopping after work.
- Lee is **doing** the ironing right now.
- She **does** the housework at the weekends.
- I have a lot of errands to **do**.
- I **did** my chores before I came to school.
- Can you **do** me a favour?
```

❗ Исключение: **make** your bed:

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- I'll **make** my bed after breakfast.
```

### Good or bad actions

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- He **did** well in his exams.
- I **did** badly in the race.
- They **did** something exciting last weekend.
- He **did** his best to pass the exam.
- Rita **does** too much for her children.
- It will **do** you good to get outdoors.
- It won't **do** you any harm.
- The storm **did** a lot of damage to the school.
```

### Sports, hobbies, and self-care

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- I need to **do** some exercise.
- Anna **does** yoga twice a day.
- Bill **does** karate in the evenings.
- She is **doing** her hair right now.
- Lara **did** my nails last weekend.
```

## Make — коллокации (создание / производство)

### Food and drink preparation

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- I'll **make** lunch.
- He **made** a cake to take to the party.
- Tim has just **made** a cup of tea.
- I **made** a sandwich to take to work.
- Sid was hungry, so he **made** a snack.
```

### Arrangements and decisions

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- I **made** an appointment to see the doctor.
- We are **making** plans for the weekend.
- I've **made** a reservation for 8:00.
- I **made** a list of the things I needed to buy.
- Ian had to **make** a difficult choice.
- It took me a long time to **make** a decision.
- I **made** a mistake in the exam.
- Why are you **making** such a mess?
```

### Speaking, sounds, and relationships

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- Jess **made** a promise to her grandmother.
- I need to **make** a phone call.
- He **made** a joke about his sister's new hairstyle.
- The teacher **made** a good suggestion.
- She **made** an excuse and left.
- The neighbours were **making** a lot of noise.
- There's no need to **make** such a fuss.
- James **made** an apology to his boss.
- We **made** a complaint to the manager.
- She's **made** friends quickly at her new school.
- I **made** a few contacts at the trade fair.
- It's not nice to **make** fun of other people.
- I need to **make** sure he's okay.
```

### Money

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- He **makes** more money than I do.
- Fred **makes** $10 an hour in his part-time job.
- She **makes** a living as a yoga teacher.
- We hope to **make** a profit from this venture.
- He **made** me an offer I couldn't refuse.
```

## Шпаргалка

| | **do** | **make** |
|---|---|---|
| Главное | выполнять, без физического результата | создавать, производить |
| Учёба / работа | do homework, do a report, do business | — |
| Дом / рутина | do the dishes, do the laundry, do chores | **make** your bed |
| Спорт / уход | do yoga, do exercise, do your hair | — |
| Еда / напитки | — | make lunch, make coffee, make a cake |
| Решения / планы | — | make a decision, make plans, make a mistake |
| Речь / отношения | — | make a promise, make friends, make sure |
| Деньги | — | make money, make a living, make a profit |
