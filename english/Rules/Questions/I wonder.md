# I wonder / No wonder

**I wonder…** = «интересно…» / «хочу узнать…» (задаёшься вопросом).  
После *wonder* — **порядок слов как в утверждении**, не как в вопросе.

![[I wonder what.png]]

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- I wonder **what he is looking at**. 🟢
- I wonder **what is he looking at**. 🔴
```

---

## 1. I wonder if / whether…

= Интересно, … ли…?

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- I wonder **if** she remembers me.
- I wonder **if** it's going to rain.
- He wondered **whether** he would pass the test.
```

*if* и *whether* ≈ одно и то же; *if* чаще (короче).  
Это **не** if-условие → *will* в придаточном **можно**:

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- I wonder if you **will** take part. 🟢 _(не правило first conditional)_
```

### Вежливая просьба

**I was wondering if you could…** — мягко попросить:

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- I **was wondering if** you could help me.
- I **was wondering if** you could look at my homework.
```

---

## 2. I wonder what / why / where / how…

Вопросительное слово + **S + V** (не *does/do/did* впереди):

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- I wonder **what** you are going to do.
- I wonder **why** she finds this book boring.
- I wonder **what time** they close.
- I was wondering **where** we were going.
```

Частая ошибка:

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- I wonder why **does she cry**? 🔴
- I wonder why **she cries**. 🟢
```

Схема: **I wonder + wh- + subject + verb**

---

## 3. No wonder

= **Неудивительно** (что…). *that* часто опускают:

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- **No wonder** you don't like swimming — you swim like a rock!
- You're kind. **No wonder** you have so many friends.
- **No wonder** she's fluent in Spanish — she grew up in Mexico.
```

---

## Шпаргалка

| Фраза | Смысл |
|---|---|
| **I wonder if/whether…** | интересно, … ли… |
| **I wonder what/why…** | интересно, что / почему… |
| **I was wondering if you could…** | вежливая просьба |
| **No wonder…** | неудивительно |

| Порядок | |
|---|---|
| ✅ | I wonder **where she lives** |
| ❌ | I wonder **where does she live** |
