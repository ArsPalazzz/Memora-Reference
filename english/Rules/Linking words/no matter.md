# no matter

SpeakOut 3B — **no matter** + относительное слово + clause: «независимо от…» / «всё равно…». То же, что *It doesn't matter*, но **сильнее акцент**.

![[no matter 3B.png]]

**Исходная страница:** [[english/Rules/Course notes/SpeakOut/no matter 3B|SpeakOut 3B (p.37)]]

---

## Смысл

> It doesn't matter when we leave. It'll be fine.  
> → **No matter when** we leave, it'll be fine.

= в любой ситуации / при любом условии — результат тот же.

---

## 1. no matter + who / whose / what / which / where / when / why / how

| Форма | Пример |
|---|---|
| **No matter whose**… | **No matter whose** pen I steal, I quickly lose it again! |
| **No matter what**… | **No matter what** time it is, we'll eat something when we arrive. |
| **No matter how**… | **No matter how** tired I am, I always manage to do some exercise after work. |
| **No matter where / when / who / which / why**… | **No matter where** you live… |

---

## 2. Время: present после no matter

После *no matter* — **present**, даже если речь о **будущем**. Future — во **второй** части:

| | Пример | Смысл |
|---|---|---|
| привычка | **No matter where you live**, I **come** and visit you. | general habit |
| будущее | **No matter where you live**, I**'ll come** and visit you. | future promise |

❌ `No matter where you **will** live…`  
✅ `No matter where you **live**…`

---

## 3. Две части — порядок любой

У *no matter* **две** части предложения; их можно **менять местами**.  
У *it doesn't matter* обычно **одна** часть + отдельное предложение:

> **No matter which** dessert you pick, I'll want some!  
> I'll want some of your dessert, **no matter which** one you pick!  
> **It doesn't matter** which dessert you pick. I'll want some!

---

## 4. no matter that…

«Неважно, что…» — факт не меняет ситуацию:

> **No matter that** you can't come tomorrow. I'll see you next week.  
> **No matter that** we've got no food in the house. We'll order a takeaway.

---

## 5. no matter what в конце

Коротко: «что бы ни случилось»:

> I'll always support you, **no matter what**.  
> I will definitely leave work by 6 p.m. and meet you tonight, **no matter what**.

---

## Шпаргалка

- **No matter** + who/whose/what/which/where/when/why/how + clause  
- После *no matter* → **present** (и для future)  
- Две части → можно **переставить**  
- *It doesn't matter* ≈ то же, но слабее / другая структура  
- **No matter that…** = неважно, что…  
- **…, no matter what.** = что бы ни было
