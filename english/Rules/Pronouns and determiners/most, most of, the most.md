# Most, most of, the most

**most** / **most of** = большинство.  
**the most** = превосходная степень (*the most* + adjective / adverb).

![[most most of the most.png]]

---

## 1. Most (+ noun)

**most** (без *the / this / that / my…*) + noun = «большинство» в общем:

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- **Most of** people can't work from home. 🔴
- **Most** people can't work from home. 🟢
- I have some bad days, but **most** days are good.
- **Most** students like online learning.
```

---

## 2. Most of (+ the / this / that / my…)

**most of** перед **the / this / that / my** и т.п. — про **конкретную** группу:

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- **Most of** students in my class practice sports. 🔴
- **Most of the** students in my class practice sports. 🟢
- **Most of this** information is not true.
```

Также **most of** + object pronoun (*us / them / you…*):

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- **Most of us** come from poor families.
- They arrested **most of them**.
```

---

## 3. Частая ошибка: не the most для «большинства»

Про majority — **most** или **most of**, **не** *the most*:

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- **The most** students like online learning. 🔴
- **Most** students like online learning. 🟢
- **The most of us** come from poor families. 🔴
- **Most of us** come from poor families. 🟢
```

---

## 4. The most (+ adjective / adverb)

**the most** перед прилагательным или наречием = **superlative**:

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- These are **the most** comfortable sofas you can buy.
- Here, you can find **the most** beautiful views in the region.
```

---

## Шпаргалка

| Форма | Значение | Когда |
|---|---|---|
| **most** + noun | большинство (в общем) | без the/this/my… |
| **most of** + the/this/my… / us/them | большинство (конкретно) | с определителем или местоимением |
| **the most** + adj/adv | превосходная степень | не для «большинства» |
