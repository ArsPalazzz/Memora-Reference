

### 1. Present

**Первичное предложение:**
- People say that he is very talented.

**С _it’s said_:**
- It is said that he is very talented._
- He is said to be very talented.

---

### 2 Continious (Verb + to be + -ing)

**Первичное:**
- People say that they are living under strict protection.

**С _it was said_:**
- It is said that they are living under strict protection.
- С инфинитивом: _They are said to be living under strict protection.

---

### 3 Past

**Первичное:**
- People say that she left early yesterday.
- People said that she had left early yesterday.

**С _it was said_:**
- It is said that she have left early yesterday.
- С инфинитивом: _She is said to have left early yesterday._
- It was said that she had left early yesterday.
- С инфинитивом: _She was said to have left early yesterday._

✅ Изменения:

- **said → was said** (past passive)
- В инфинитиве добавляется **to have + past participle** для действия в прошлом.

С present perfect такая же конструкция

### 4 Future

**Первичное:**
- People will say that the project is important.

**С _it will be said_:**

- It will be said that the project is important._
- С инфинитивом: _The project will be said to be important._

✅ Изменения:

- **will say → will be said** (future passive)
- С инфинитивом: **to be** сохраняет смысл качества/статуса.
