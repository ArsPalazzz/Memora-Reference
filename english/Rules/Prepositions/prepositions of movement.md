# Prepositions of movement

Предлоги **направления** после глаголов движения (*walk, run, go, drive, fly…*), иногда после других глаголов или существительных (*the path **to** the beach*).

![[prepositions of movement.png]]

---

## 1. Основные предлоги

| Предлог | Идея | Примеры |
|---|---|---|
| **to** | к цели | I took them **to** the beach. |
| **from** | откуда | She comes **from** Japan. |
| **into** | внутрь | I walked **into** the room. |
| **out of** | наружу | They ran **out of** the room. |
| **on** | на поверхность / транспорт | got **on** the bus; put it **on** the table |
| **off** | с поверхности | jumped **off** the table; fell **off** his bike |
| **up / down** | вверх / вниз | **up** the hill; **down** the stairs |
| **along** | вдоль | walked **along** the beach |
| **through** | сквозь | drove **through** the tunnel |
| **across** | через (с одной стороны на другую) | swam **across** the river |
| **towards** | в сторону | walked **towards** me |
| **over** | через / над | jumped **over** the fence |
| **under** | под | drove **under** the bridge |
| **around** | вокруг / за угол | went **around** the corner |
| **past** | мимо | walked **past** us |

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- I'd like to go **to** the cinema.
- I ran **from** home **to** the gym.
- **Get into** the car.
- Jessica swam **across** the river.
- He jumped **over** the fence.
- The police drove **past** our building.
```

## 2. Get on/off vs get into/out of

| | Транспорт |
|---|---|
| **get into / out of** | car, taxi, van |
| **get on / off** | bus, train, plane; bike, motorbike |

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- I have to **get off** the bus at the next stop.
- He stopped and **got out of** the car.
```

---

## 3. Как добраться: by… / on foot vs глагол / take

Два способа сказать одно и то же:

### by + транспорт / on foot

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- I go to school **on foot**.
- I go to work **by car**.
- I went to Zurich **by plane**.
- I went to the airport **by taxi**.
```

### глагол движения / take + транспорт

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- I **walk** to school. _(= on foot)_
- I **drive** to work. _(= by car)_
- I **flew** to Zurich. _(= by plane)_
- I **took** a taxi/a bus/a train to the airport.
```

## Шпаргалка

| | Формула |
|---|---|
| машина / такси | **get into / out of** |
| автобус / поезд / самолёт / bike | **get on / off** |
| способ | **by** car/bus…; **on** foot |
| или глагол | **drive / walk / fly / take** a bus… |
