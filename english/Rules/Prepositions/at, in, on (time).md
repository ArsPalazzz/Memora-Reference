# at / in / on (time)

Предлоги времени: **at** — точка во времени, **in** — длинный период, **on** — день / дата.

![[at in on time.png]]

---

## 1. at

С **точным временем**, приёмами пищи, bedtime и похожими «моментами дня»:

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- **at** 3 o'clock, **at** 10.30 am, **at** noon
- **at** dinnertime, **at** bedtime, **at** sunrise, **at** sunset
- **at** the moment
```

Частые выражения с **at**:

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- I don't usually work **at the weekend**.
- I stay with my family **at Christmas** / **at Easter**.
- We finished the test **at the same time**.
- He's not home **at present**. Try later.
- He's not home **at the moment**.
```

---

## 2. in

С **месяцами, сезонами, годами, десятилетиями, веками** и длинными периодами вообще:

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- **in** May, **in** the summer, **in** 1990
- **in** the 1990s, **in** the 20th century
- **in** the Ice Age, **in** the past / **in** the future
```

---

## 3. on

С **днями и датами** (включая праздники как конкретный день):

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- **on** Sunday, **on** Tuesday mornings
- **on** 6 March, **on** 25 December 2010
- **on** Christmas Day, **on** Independence Day
- **on** my birthday, **on** New Year's Eve
```

Часть **конкретного** дня → тоже **on**:

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- **on** Monday morning, **on** Friday evening
- **on** Saturday night, **on** Sunday afternoon
```

---

## 4. Parts of the day

Общие части дня — **in**, но **night** — исключение (**at**):

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- **in** the morning
- **in** the afternoon
- **in** the evening
- **at** night 🟢
```

---

## 5. last / next / every / this

После **last, next, every, this** предлоги **at / in / on** **не** ставим:

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- I went to London **last** June. 🟢
- I went to London **in last** June. 🔴
- He's coming back **next** Tuesday. 🟢
- He's coming back **on next** Tuesday. 🔴
- I go home **every** Easter. 🟢
- I go home **at every** Easter. 🔴
- We'll call you **this** evening. 🟢
- We'll call you **in this** evening. 🔴
```

---

## Шпаргалка

| Предлог | Когда | Примеры |
|---|---|---|
| **at** | точка во времени; weekend; Christmas/Easter; night; present/moment | at 4 o'clock, at noon, at the weekend, at night |
| **in** | месяц / сезон / год / век / длинный период; morning/afternoon/evening | in May, in 1990, in the morning |
| **on** | день / дата; часть конкретного дня | on Tuesday, on 15 June, on Monday morning |
| **—** | last / next / every / this | last Friday, next weekend, every Easter, this evening |
