# Reported / indirect speech

Когда передаём чужие слова или мысли, часто используем **reported speech** (indirect speech). Если говорящий и время **другие**, меняем **времена глаголов** и **местоимения**.

---

## 1. Базовая схема

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- Sally: 'I **don't have** time.' ⇒ Sally said (that) she **didn't have** time.
- Peter: 'I **am** tired.' ⇒ He said (that) he **was** tired.
```

---

## 2. Omission of **that**

После *say, think* и похожих глаголов **that** часто **опускают**:

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- She said (that) she was late. = She said she was late.
- I thought (that) I would get the job. = I thought I would get the job.
```

---

## 3. **say** or **tell**?

| | Формула |
|---|---|
| **say** | say **something** (to somebody) |
| **tell** | tell **somebody something** |

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- They **said me** (that) they would help me. 🔴
- They **told me** (that) they would help me. 🟢
- He **told** (that) he didn't have a car. 🔴
- He **said** (that) he didn't have a car. 🟢
```

---

## 4. Tense changes

Если человек **сказал что-то в прошлом**, а мы **сейчас** передаём это другому — времена обычно **сдвигаются назад**:

![[indirect speech tense changes.png]]

| Direct speech | Reported speech |
|---|---|
| present simple | past simple |
| present continuous | past continuous |
| past simple | past perfect |
| present perfect | past perfect |
| **will** | **would** |
| **must** | **had to** |

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- 'I **am** exhausted.' ⇒ He said (that) he **was** exhausted.
- 'I **am studying**.' ⇒ She told me (that) she **was studying**.
- 'I **loved** the concert.' ⇒ He told me (that) he **had loved** the concert.
- 'I **have** never **been** to Rome.' ⇒ She said (that) she **had** never **been** to Rome.
- 'I **will help** you.' ⇒ He said (that) he **would help** me.
- 'You **must pay** attention.' ⇒ She told me (that) I **had to pay** attention.
```

---

## 5. Pronoun changes

В прямой речи говорящий использует **I / me / my / we** и **you / your**.  
В indirect speech — **he / she / his / her** про говорящего и **I / me / my** про нас, слушающих:

```ad-example
title: Example
color: 78, 121, 237
- 'I **will help you**.' ⇒ He said (that) he **would help me**.
- 'That's **my** pen.' ⇒ She said (that) it was **her** pen.
- 'I need **your** help.' ⇒ She said (that) she needed **my** help.
```

---

## Шпаргалка

| Что меняется | Прямая → indirect |
|---|---|
| **Время** | present → past; past → past perfect; will → would; must → had to |
| **Местоимения** | I → he/she; you → I/me; my → his/her; your → my |
| **that** | можно опустить после say / think |
| **say / tell** | tell **кому**; say **что** (без «кому» как объекта) |
